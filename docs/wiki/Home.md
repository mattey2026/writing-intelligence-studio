# ✍️ Writing Intelligence Studio Wiki

*Think deeper. Write better. Publish with confidence.*

Writing Intelligence Studio is a Claude plugin that works like a small editorial team: an intake editor, a researcher, an author, a sceptical senior editor, a copy editor and a fact-checker. It helps you plan, research, draft, challenge, edit, verify, review and publish substantial writing, and it keeps you in control of every change.

## Design principles

1. Purpose before prose.
2. Research before factual claims.
3. Original thinking before generic summaries.
4. Human editorial quality before formulaic output.
5. Verifiable references before unsupported assertions.
6. You control your voice, your data and every change.
7. Honest about limits: no promises about AI detectors or plagiarism checkers.
