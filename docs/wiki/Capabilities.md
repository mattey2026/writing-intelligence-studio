# Capabilities

## What it can do

### Planning and writing
- Understand a plain-language request and infer format, purpose, audience, tone, depth, reading level, length, perspective and destination.
- Ask only the questions that would change the result (at most three).
- Write essays, articles, blog posts, opinion pieces, news analysis, technical guides, white papers, business reports, strategy papers, newsletters, LinkedIn posts, tutorials, case studies, speeches, scripts, book chapters, personal reflections and research summaries.
- Apply seven editorial personas, alone or combined.
- Write at three depths and four reading levels.
- Write in your own voice from a saved voice profile.
- Build on your earlier pieces, or combine several into a long-form report.

### Evidence and integrity
- Cite only sources you provide, using `[S1]` markers and a numbered reference list in six styles.
- Flag every unsupported factual claim `[needs source]`.
- Never invent references, statistics, quotations, URLs or personal anecdotes; insert `[Add your own example: ...]` instead.
- Fact-check each claim against your sources.
- Suggest documented real-world examples, publications and YouTube videos, each with a verification link.
- Embed YouTube videos as sources.

### Editing and quality
- Challenge drafts with issues, counterarguments and missing context.
- Apply edits to a selection or the whole piece, shown as tracked changes.
- Measure reading ease, grade level, sentence length, passive voice, dashes, paragraph length, AI-style phrases and repetitive openings.
- Detect wording copied from your sources and earlier drafts.
- Score editorial quality on ten dimensions with the highest-value edits.

### Publishing
- Generate search titles, meta descriptions, keywords and reader questions.
- Suggest 5 to 8 LinkedIn hashtags.
- Recommend posting days and times by platform and region, converted to your time zone.
- Export Markdown, HTML (with embedded illustrations and videos) and plain text.
- Repurpose into a LinkedIn post, newsletter, executive summary or thread.
- Create original vector illustrations with alt text and captions.

### Library and history
- Keep a private library of documents, sources, voice profiles and versions.
- Search by exact words or by meaning; find related documents automatically.
- Save, compare and restore versions; undo and redo.
- Show estimated token usage per document.

## What it doesn't do (yet)

| Limitation | Workaround today |
|---|---|
| The workspace can't browse the web | Ask Claude in chat to research and save sources to your library |
| No photographic or painted images | Copy the provided prompt into an image generator |
| No Word or PDF export | Print the HTML export to PDF, or ask Claude in chat to convert it |
| No automatic posting to LinkedIn or other platforms | Copy and paste; use the posting-time guidance |
| Originality check covers only your sources and drafts | Run Turnitin, Copyleaks or Grammarly before formal submission |
| Keywords are chosen without live search-volume data | Check volumes in your SEO tool of choice |
| Token counts are estimates | Check your Claude usage page for exact figures |
| Single user only; no shared team workspaces | Share exported files |
