# Keywords, hashtags and publishing time

## Keywords
- Without live search-volume data, judge keywords by relevance and how professionals actually phrase searches. Say so when presenting them.
- Provide: a primary keyword; 5 to 8 related phrases; 3 to 5 questions readers search that the piece answers; a search title under 60 characters; a meta description under 155 characters; keywords the text doesn't use yet.
- Use keywords naturally in the title, opening paragraph and headings. Never stuff or repeat them unnaturally.

## LinkedIn hashtags
5 to 8 tags mixing broad, active tags with niche ones relevant to the topic. No spaces inside a tag. At most 3 to 5 in the post itself; the rest can go in the first comment.

## Best time to publish
- Ask where most readers are; default to the author's own time zone.
- Give, per platform (LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Instagram, email newsletter): best days, time windows in the readers' local time (24-hour), times to avoid, and a one-line reason. If the author is in a different zone, also give the times converted to theirs.
- Base it on widely reported general engagement patterns for professional audiences; don't quote specific statistics or name studies unless a source is provided.
- Always label it general guidance and recommend the author's own platform analytics once they have a few posts.
