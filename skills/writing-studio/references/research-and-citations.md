# Research and citations

## Source hierarchy
1. Primary sources and original research
2. Official documentation and institutional publications
3. Peer-reviewed research
4. Reputable journalism and recognized industry analysis
5. Expert commentary and other credible secondary sources

## Research workflow
1. Break the topic into the questions the piece must answer.
2. List the claims that need verification.
3. Search for current, authoritative sources (when a search tool is available).
4. Compare independent sources; note disagreements, outdated information and uncertainty.
5. Draft from the evidence.
6. Verify names, dates, figures, quotations and links.
7. Link each important claim to its supporting source.

## Record for each source
Title; author or organization; publication date; URL or DOI; access date; type (web page, journal article, report, official documentation, video, book); the passage or facts it supports; whether the author has opened and confirmed it.

## Real-world examples
Use only real, documented cases. Prefer examples from the source list. A well-known case from general knowledge must carry `[needs source]` until a source is added. Never invent companies, people, products or events.

## Videos
A YouTube video can be a source (type: video). In Markdown, embed it by putting its URL alone on its own line, followed by an italic caption ending with its citation marker.

## Citation formats
- **APA:** Author. (Year). Title. Publisher. URL
- **MLA:** Author. "Title." Publisher, Date, URL.
- **Chicago:** Author. "Title." Publisher, Date. URL.
- **Harvard:** Author (Year) Title. Publisher. Available at: URL (Accessed: date).
- **IEEE:** [n] Author, "Title," Publisher, Date. [Online]. Available: URL
- **Inline links:** Title, Publisher: URL

Number references in order of first citation. Mark any reference the author hasn't confirmed as "not yet verified".

## Never
Invent references, DOIs, URLs, quotations, statistics or source details. If a source can't be verified, mark it for review instead of presenting it as verified.
