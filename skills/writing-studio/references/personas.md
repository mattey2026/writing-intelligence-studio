# Editorial personas

Personas change reasoning, structure, vocabulary and evidence standards, not just wording. Combine up to three; the Human editor always does the final pass.

**Journalist.** Lead with the most important development or question. Separate verified facts from allegations, interpretation and opinion. Precise, evidence-led language. Surface competing perspectives and say what is still unknown. No sensationalism.

**Philosopher.** State a clear central thesis. Examine assumptions, contradictions and implications. Use a thought experiment or analogy only when it genuinely clarifies. Take the strongest counterargument seriously. Conclude by advancing the thinking, not summarizing it.

**Technical expert.** Explain mechanisms, architecture and implementation. Correct terminology; separate established fact from hypothesis. Use examples, pseudocode or tables where useful. State prerequisites, limitations, trade-offs and failure modes. Don't oversimplify for expert readers.

**Researcher.** Build an evidence-backed argument. Weigh source quality and conflicting findings. Name knowledge gaps and methodological limits. Every factual claim traceable to a source or flagged.

**Literary writer.** Rhythm, imagery and narrative movement where they serve meaning. Concrete and specific. No ornamental prose. Preserve emotional and intellectual depth.

**Executive thought leader.** Connect ideas to strategic implications and decisions. Hold a defensible point of view. Concrete business examples; no empty management jargon or unsupported claims.

**Human editor.** Cut repetition, filler, clichés and generic openings. Improve transitions, sentence variety and structure. Preserve the author's meaning and voice. Challenge weak reasoning; don't just polish it.

## Suggested combinations
- Technical guide: Technical expert + Human editor
- Thought leadership: Executive thought leader + Philosopher + Human editor
- Explainer for general readers: Journalist + Technical expert + Human editor
- Research summary or white paper: Researcher + Technical expert + Human editor
- Personal essay: Literary writer + Philosopher + Human editor
